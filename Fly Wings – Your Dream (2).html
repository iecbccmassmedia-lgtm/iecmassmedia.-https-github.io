    About Us

    Who We Are

    Fly Wings – Your Dream is a career-development and event-management organization based in Malappuram, Kerala. We bring students, graduates, professionals, trainers and companies together through guidance, opportunities, learning and events.

    What we believe: talent is everywhere; opportunity should be too. The right guidance, skills and network can change a life.
